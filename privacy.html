Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Volaire collects no data. Your files travel only between your own iPhone and your own Mac, and nothing ever leaves your devices.

This policy covers the Volaire app for iPhone and Mac, including its Share Extension and the optional Volaire Pro upgrade. Volaire is made by Cole Stegman ("we", "us").

What we collect

Nothing. Volaire has no accounts, no sign in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information, usage data, diagnostics, or identifiers. We never see your files, their names, or how you use the app.

How your files travel

When you send a file, it goes directly from your iPhone to the Mac you paired with, over your local network or a direct peer to peer connection between the two devices. It does not pass through any server, ours or anyone else's.

  • Pairing. The first time, your Mac shows a 4 digit code and you type it on your iPhone. The two devices use it to create a shared secret key. The key is stored in the secure keychain on each device and never leaves them.
  • Every transfer. Each connection is authenticated with that key, and every file and every live drag update is encrypted end to end between your two devices. Other devices on the same network cannot read or alter what you send.
  • On your Mac. Received files are saved to the folder you choose (by default Downloads/Volaire). They are yours, stored like any other file on your Mac.

Local network access

Volaire asks for Local Network permission on your iPhone and Mac. It uses this only to find your paired Mac nearby and to send files to it. Volaire does not scan, collect, or report anything about other devices on your network.

Photos access

When you pick a photo or video, Volaire receives only the item you chose, through the system photo picker. It does not browse or read the rest of your library.

If you use Send and Free Up (Volaire Pro), Volaire asks for permission to remove that original from your iPhone photo library after your Mac confirms the file landed safely. Your iPhone then shows its own confirmation before anything is deleted, so nothing is removed without your approval. Volaire keeps only a running total of space freed, stored on your device.

Smart Names, on device

Volaire can suggest a friendly name for a photo, like "Sunset Beach, Oct 20". These names are created entirely on your iPhone using built in, on device image analysis and the date the photo was taken. No image or name is sent anywhere for this, and no network connection is used. You can rename a file before sending, or rename it later from the history on your Mac.

History and counters

Your recent transfers and your lifetime counters (files sent, space freed) are stored only on your own devices. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

Volaire Pro is a one time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. Volaire never sees your payment details, your Apple Account, or your billing information. The app only learns whether Pro is unlocked, so it can turn those features on. Restoring a purchase also goes through Apple.

Third parties

Volaire contains no third party code that collects data: no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social SDKs. We share no information with anyone, because we have none to share.

Children

Volaire is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no data, there is nothing for us to hold, review, or delete.

Your choices

You can turn off Local Network or Photos access at any time in Settings on your iPhone or in System Settings on your Mac. You can forget a paired device from Volaire on either side, which deletes its key from that device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change ever affected how your information is handled, we would explain it here before it takes effect.
