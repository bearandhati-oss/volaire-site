Help for Volaire on iPhone and Mac

Open Volaire on your Mac, open it on your iPhone, pair once with a 4 digit code. After that, every file is one flick.

Getting started

  1. Install Volaire on your Mac and open it. Its icon appears in the menu bar at the top of the screen. There is no window or Dock icon, that is expected.
  2. Install Volaire on your iPhone and open it.
  3. When asked, allow Local Network access on both devices. Volaire needs it to find your Mac.

How pairing works

  1. On your iPhone, tap your Mac in the list.
  2. Your Mac shows a 4 digit code. Type it on your iPhone.
  3. That is it. The two devices create a private key, save it securely on each side, and reconnect on their own from now on.

If a code is mistyped, your Mac shows a fresh one. Just type the new code. To pair a different Mac, open Settings on your iPhone (the gear on the main screen) and choose Pair a Different Mac.

Choose where your Mac is

Right after pairing, Volaire asks Where is your Mac? Pick Left, Above, or Right, based on where your Mac sits compared with the iPhone in your hand. Your files then fly in that direction, and they appear through the matching edge of your Mac screen.

To change it later, tap the gear on the main screen of your iPhone and choose a new side under Where is your Mac?

Sending a file

  • Pick a photo, video, or document. It appears as a card.
  • Drag the card toward your Mac. It slides off your iPhone and onto your Mac at the same time.
  • Let go past halfway, or give it a quick flick, and it lands. Let go before halfway and it springs back, nothing is sent.
  • Prefer not to swipe? Use the Send button, or the Send to Mac action with VoiceOver.
  • From another app, use the share sheet and choose Volaire.

Files land in Downloads/Volaire on your Mac unless you chose a different folder. Click the Volaire menu bar icon and choose Open Volaire Folder to find them.

Troubleshooting

My iPhone cannot find my Mac

  • Volaire must be open on your Mac. Check for its icon in the menu bar. If it is not there, open Volaire from your Applications folder.
  • Keep both devices close. The easiest setup is both on the same Wi-Fi network. Volaire can also connect the two directly when they are near each other, with Wi-Fi turned on for both.
  • Allow Local Network on your iPhone. Open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Local Network, and turn on Volaire.
  • Allow Local Network on your Mac. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Local Network, and turn on Volaire.
  • Check the firewall on your Mac. If the firewall is on, open System Settings, then Network, then Firewall, then Options, and make sure Volaire is allowed to receive incoming connections.
  • Guest or office networks sometimes block devices from seeing each other. Try a home network or a personal hotspot.

The file did not arrive

  • If you let go before halfway, the card springs back and nothing is sent. Drag further, or flick.
  • Make sure there is free space on your Mac and that the save folder still exists. Choose Open Volaire Folder from the menu bar icon to check.
  • The free version sends files up to 500 MB each. Volaire Pro removes the limit.

Pairing keeps failing

  • Type the code currently shown on your Mac. A wrong code makes the Mac show a new one.
  • On your Mac, open the Volaire menu, forget the old pairing under Paired devices, then pair again from your iPhone.

Volaire Pro

Volaire Pro is a one time purchase that unlocks Send and Free Up, unlimited file size, full history, and custom save folders on your Mac, including a folder per file type. Buy it once and it works on both your iPhone and your Mac.

Restore purchases

On a new device or after reinstalling, open Volaire, go to Settings, then Volaire Pro, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Send and Free Up

With Pro, Send and Free Up removes the original photo or video from your iPhone, but only after your Mac confirms the file landed safely. Your iPhone always asks you to confirm the deletion. Removed items go to Recently Deleted in your photo library, where you can recover them for a limited time.
